# One incident, told in four lines

**Situation.** In spring 2024 a coach operator went bankrupt a week before the Easter
departures; thirty-one customers of the agency had paid for seats that no longer existed.

**What I did.** I listed every affected file in one spreadsheet the same morning, called
each customer in order of departure date, and wrote the same three-line e-mail to all of
them: what happened, what the agency would do, when they would hear from me again.

**What it cost.** Two days on the phone instead of selling, and three customers who
cancelled their whole trip rather than wait.

**What I changed.** The cancellation checklist: every step got an owner and a date, and
the refund delay on the following cases fell from three weeks to eight working days.
