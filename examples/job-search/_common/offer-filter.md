# Offer filter

An offer is prepared only if it passes this filter. The owner wrote the criteria; the
assistant applies them and records the result in `applications.md` under notes.

## An offer must meet all three

1. Customers are answered in writing as well as on the phone (e-mail, chat, tickets).
2. Lyon, or fully remote with at most one trip a month.
3. A fixed salary; a bonus may come on top of it, never instead of it.

## An offer is excluded by any one

1. Scripted outbound calls, or a call centre measured on calls per hour.
2. Commission-only pay, or a "trial period" without a contract.
3. A role that requires selling a product the owner would not buy.
