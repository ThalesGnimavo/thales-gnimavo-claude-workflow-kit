# E-mail templates

> No bracket survives in a sent e-mail. Fill every `[placeholder]`, re-read, then send
> from your own mailbox. Nothing in this project sends anything.

## 1. Application e-mail (letter and CV attached)

Subject: Application — [exact title as published] — Léa Fontaine

Dear [named person],

Please find attached my application for the position of [exact title as published]
[reference, if any]. My letter says why this role and why your company; my CV gives the
facts behind it.

I am available [availability] for a call or a meeting.

Kind regards,
Léa Fontaine
[phone]

## 2. Follow-up, ten working days after sending

Subject: Re: Application — [exact title as published] — Léa Fontaine

Dear [named person],

I sent you my application for [exact title as published] on [date sent]. I remain
interested in the role and available for a conversation at your convenience.

Kind regards,
Léa Fontaine
